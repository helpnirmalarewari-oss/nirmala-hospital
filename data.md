## 1. Overview / Basic Introduction

Nirmala Multispeciality Hospital is a **women-owned, private multispeciality hospital** located in Rewari district, Haryana. It positions itself as a **"Super Specialty Hospital with state-of-the-art infrastructure & path-breaking technology"** (official Facebook/Instagram tagline), operating on the mission **"Your health is our Priority."**

The hospital is run primarily by a husband-wife doctor duo:
- **Dr. Ravi Yadav** — Internal Medicine & Diabetology
- **Dr. Jyoti Yadav** — Obstetrics & Gynaecology

It has built a strong local reputation for mother-child care, general physician services, and ICU/emergency care, and is well reviewed across multiple local listing platforms.
